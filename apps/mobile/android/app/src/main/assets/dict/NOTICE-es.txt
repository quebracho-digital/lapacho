Spanish word list: es.txt

Derived from hermitdave/FrequencyWords (2018 Spanish list, es_50k.txt),
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords — word frequencies counted over
the OpenSubtitles corpus.

Filtered for Lapacho: entries that are not plain lowercase Spanish letters
(a-z plus áéíóúüñ) are dropped, as are one-letter words other than a, e, o, u
and y. Nothing else is changed; the second column is the original count.
