French word list: fr.txt

Derived from hermitdave/FrequencyWords (2018 list, fr_50k.txt),
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords — word frequencies counted over
the OpenSubtitles corpus.

Built by dictionaries/build.py (fr recipe, see docs/DICTIONARIES.md):
only plain lowercase words (a-z plus à â æ ç é è ê ë î ï ô œ ù û ü ÿ) are
kept — elisions (c', l') and hyphenated words go, since the keyboard starts a
new word after the mark — and of the one-letter ones a, à and y. A word that
lost an accent (ca, deja) and is under a tenth as common as the accented
spelling gives its count to it and is dropped. The #rows line is AZERTY.
The `#` lines at the top are Lapacho's dictionary header; the second column
is the original count (plus any merged one).

This file's SHA-256 is listed in the app as an official dictionary
(OFFICIAL_DICTIONARIES in Predict.kt); a test fails if the two drift apart.
